Tanzania Nzuri — three directions

Three complete, click-through versions of the new site. Each carries every page from the brief — home, experiences and places, journeys with the Real Trip Cost, the Kilimanjaro route compare, the journey builder, the Trip Designer, the travel guide, the team. Open one and walk it on your phone as well as your laptop. The palette menu in the black bar at the top of every page switches colour schemes; Direction default is the one each design was drawn in.
