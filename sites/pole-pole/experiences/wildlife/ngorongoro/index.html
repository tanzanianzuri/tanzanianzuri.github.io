Ngorongoro Crater.

A collapsed volcano with a whole ecosystem living inside it — and the best odds of a black rhino in Tanzania.

1,800 mBest All yearThe Wilderness
Add Ngorongoro Crater to my journey Ask a guide

What you do here

Four things worth the day.

The crater floor

Six hours down on the floor: lion, elephant, hippo, flamingo, and the best black-rhino odds in the country.

The rim at dawn

Camps and lodges sit on the rim at 2,250 m. It is cold, it is often in cloud, and the view when it clears is the reason people come.

Maasai live here

Ngorongoro is a conservation area, not a park — Maasai graze cattle on the highlands, which is why it looks the way it does.

Olduvai on the way

The gorge and its museum sit between the crater and the Serengeti — an hour that puts the whole landscape in order.
