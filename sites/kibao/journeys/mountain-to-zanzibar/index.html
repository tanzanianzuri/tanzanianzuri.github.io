Day by day

12 days, in order.

  1. Day01

    Arrive and brief

    Transfer, gear check and the mountain briefing with the guide who will lead you up.

    Moshi
  2. Day02

    Lemosho, day one

    Into the forest. The first of seven days on the mountain — the itinerary is the Lemosho route above.

    Kilimanjaro
  3. Day03

    Shira and the plateau

    Two days across the plateau, climbing high and sleeping low.

    Kilimanjaro
  4. Day04

    Summit night

    Stella Point at dawn, Uhuru Peak at 5,895 m, and the long descent to camp.

    Uhuru Peak
  5. Day05

    Down, and a rest day

    Mweka Gate, certificates, and a day that is nothing but a pool and a long lunch.

    Moshi
  6. Day06

    Fly to the plains

    A light aircraft from Arusha to the central Serengeti — no long drive after the mountain.

    Serengeti
  7. Day07

    Two days with the herds

    Full days out, an afternoon in camp, and dinner under the stars.

    Serengeti
  8. Day08

    The crater floor

    Down at sunrise for the descent, and the Maasai morning on the rim.

    Ngorongoro
  9. Day09

    Salt after dust

    Fly from the Serengeti to Zanzibar. Stone Town in the afternoon, and the spice farms if you want them.

    Zanzibar
  10. Day10

    The north coast

    Three nights on the beach. Snorkelling, a dhow at sunset, and nothing on the schedule.

    Nungwi
  11. Day11

    Nothing at all

    The day the trip is for.

    Nungwi
  12. Day12

    Home

    A morning swim and the transfer to Zanzibar airport.

    Zanzibar
