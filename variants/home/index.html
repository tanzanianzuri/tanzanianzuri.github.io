The argument each one makes

DirectionThe claimBest atWhat it costsBreaks when
A · Pole pole The brand's core story is the climb — make scrolling the homepage an ascent. Memorable and playful without gimmick; the zone bands give long pages rhythm, and the facts are real. Section order is tied to altitude; adding a section means choosing its zone. The altimeter needs a light touch on mobile. The inner pages copy the zone metaphor — it belongs to the homepage only.
B · Tanzanite Every competitor is khaki and orange; own the one colour no other country can claim. Premium and honeymoon markets; stands apart from Tranquil, Lion King and Afromaxx at a glance. Deepest colour of the five. The least "safari" at first sight — the photography has to carry the wildlife. Budget travellers read violet-and-facets as expensive and leave before seeing a price.
C · Kibao The Tanzanian design every visitor already photographs is the park sign — make the site wayfinding. Kilimanjaro expertise; instantly recognisable to anyone who has climbed. The elevation profile is honest data. The heaviest look; timber panels on every section can feel rugged rather than premium. The business leans to beach and honeymoon — signage says "trek" louder than "Zanzibar".
